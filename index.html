HOW TO USE EMA SCANNER

200 EMA SYSTEM

200 EMA CLOSE — Green background = price above 200 EMA (bullish). Red = below (bearish). This is your primary trend filter.

200 EMA HIGH — APR = price approaching the top of the 200 EMA band. IN = inside the band. 🫣 = fallen below close.

200 EMA LOW — Shows % above the low when between close and low. 🚫 = price has broken below the 200 EMA low — no entry.

100 EMA H/L BAND

APR = approaching from above within 2%. ▲ % LOW = inside band, showing how far above the low support you are.

5 EMA LOW %

Green background = price above the 5 EMA Low — potential entry zone. 🚫 = below the 5 EMA Low — wait.

200 EMA SUPPORT / 5 EMA CONT

SUPPORT = EMA holding, conditions met for entry. WATCH = approaching key level, prepare.
