HOW TO USE EMA SCANNER

READING THE CARDS

200 EMA CLOSE — Green bg = above 200 EMA (bull). Red = below (bear). Primary trend filter.

200 EMA HIGH — APR = approaching top of band. IN = inside band. Dark red cell = fallen through.

200 EMA LOW — Active between close and low. Shows % above last support. Dark red = broken.

100 EMA H/L — APR ▼ = approaching within 2%. ▲ % LOW = inside band, % from low support.

5 EMA LOW % — Green bg = above 5 EMA Low (entry zone). Dark red = below, no entry.

5 EMA HTF BAND — Next higher TF 5 EMA zone. Green = near low (best entry). Yellow = approaching. Dark red = broken.

VOLUME — HIGH = institutions active. AVERAGE = normal. LOW = quiet.

200 EMA SUPPORT / 5 EMA CONTINUED — SUPPORT = conditions met. WATCH = approaching key level.

USING THE SCANNER

Use ROW TOGGLE ON/OFF and TIMEFRAME TOGGLE ON/OFF to show/hide rows or columns.

Use BULL / BEAR / SUPPORT filters to narrow down cards.

Click ☆ star on any card to add to master watchlist. Filter by ★ WATCHLIST to view only starred stocks.

Click any ticker name to open directly in TradingView.
